Real NASA Science & Open Data Sources

1. DONKI (Space Weather Database)

Provides live and historical data on Solar Flares and Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs). In our game, DONKI data dictates solar radiation intensity and communications disturbances.

2. RadLab & NASA OSDR (Biological Science)

RadLab records physical dosimeter measurements aboard the ISS, while the Open Science Data Repository studies cosmic radiation impacts on astronaut bone density and immune defense.

3. CSA Space Food Production Systems

From the Canadian Space Agency & NASA Deep Space Food Challenge: models hydroponics, water cycling, and LED energy consumption required to grow fresh greens in microgravity and planetary outposts.

4. Mars Trek & Rover Science (Perseverance & Curiosity)

Real Jezero Crater environmental logs including atmospheric pressure, dust opacity (Tau index), and solar insolance drops caused by seasonal Martian dust storms.
